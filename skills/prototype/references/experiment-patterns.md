# Experiment Patterns

Choose the smallest pattern that can answer the question. Combine patterns only when one experiment still answers one question.

## Causal isolation

Change one suspected cause while keeping other relevant conditions comparable. Observe whether the system behavior changes as the hypothesis predicts.

## Representation or model

Build a representation of the relevant system behavior. Exercise the conditions that distinguish the plausible answers and make the result observable.

## Alternative forms

Compare materially different candidates against the same intended outcome. Keep their surrounding conditions equivalent enough that the observed difference informs the decision.

## Rehearsal or walkthrough

Rehearse the relevant behavior without creating the live commitment being evaluated. Observe where behavior diverges from the intended outcome.

## Bounded pilot

Pilot the intervention inside a boundary that can be stopped and restored by an actor with authority. Compare the resulting outcome with the baseline and observe any cost transferred to related actors or systems.

## Physical mock or staged trial

Use a safely resettable setup. Keep people and live equipment outside material hazard, and stop when the evidence would require crossing the reversible boundary.

## Agent sandbox

Replace external inputs and effects with disposable substitutes. Inspect the agent's behavior and outputs while credentials remain outside the experiment boundary.
