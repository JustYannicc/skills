# Inline route and mid-work classifier

Read this reference when any message arrives during active work. Inline compresses representation; it preserves the complete lifecycle.

## Inline contract

Keep these meanings represented in the conversation, even when they remain implicit and the user-visible result is one sentence:

- one accepted Outcome and owner;
- the accepted request as semantic Specification and one implicit Ticket;
- material assumptions, Constraints, Authority, and result revision;
- the current phase, evidence trail, and any child return;
- the proof seam, immediate Review, effect evidence, and parent terminal check.

For trivial reversible work, preserve the source, apply the exact accepted operation, inspect the result, return only what was requested, and complete the implicit Ticket and parent Review in the same interaction. Triviality reduces ceremony, never systems judgment, responsibility, Authority, or proof.

Before accepting a direct request for a later phase, verify that phase's minimum contract. Missing material input returns the smallest discriminating question; it does not authorize invention.

## Classify every mid-work clause

Capture the exact incoming message and classify each operative clause by its effect on the active contract. A message may contain more than one class. Process compatible clauses together; a replacement or unrelated switch changes which Outcome later clauses address.

| Class | Test | Required route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clarification | Resolves an existing ambiguity without changing accepted Outcome, scope, Constraints, Authority, proof, or treatment of existing state. | Append the clarified fact or meaning to the evidence trail and continue at the same Specification and result revisions. |
| Extension or Constraint change | Adds or changes accepted Outcome, scope, supported conditions, Constraint, interface, Authority, proof, or legacy treatment. | Append a change proposal, create the next Specification or result revision when material, impact-analyze, and reroute only affected work. |
| Correction | States or proves that an earlier interpretation, evidence claim, result, or completion assertion was false under the original contract. | Append a correction event, preserve history, invalidate the false evidence, reopen affected responsibility, and recover from the failed seam. |
| Authorized override or informed exception | Explicitly asks to bypass a safeguard; asserted Authority may still need verification. | Verify Authority. If valid, record the skipped protection, plausible consequence, affected scope and duration, and Review, reversal, or recovery trigger while keeping remaining safeguards and proof active. Otherwise preserve it as a blocked proposal. |
| Explicit replacement or supersession | Explicitly replaces the active Outcome or accepted contract rather than extending it. | Record predecessor, partial effects, retained evidence, and every child disposition; establish or join the successor only after required recovery and ownership are explicit. |
| Unrelated switch | Does not advance, clarify, correct, replace, or share a material contract with the active Outcome. | Preserve exact continuation first, then start or join the other Outcome. Resume the original from its recorded pickup point when requested. |

When the class or Authority is materially ambiguous, preserve the plausible classes and ask the smallest question that changes the route. A requester's willingness to accept risk is evidence of Preference, not proof of Authority.

## Apply a material change

For a material extension, Constraint change, exception, or replacement:

1. Preserve the baseline revision and append the source message, classification, proposed revision, rationale, Authority, and expected effect.
2. Trace the changed contract slice through its dependency closure. Inspect only present or plausibly affected Tickets, completed work, dependencies, evidence, Review, approvals, effects, interfaces, current state, and legacy state.
3. Mark each item affected or unaffected with the reason. Invalidate only affected evidence, Review, approval, or completion. A material result edit always creates a new revision and invalidates Review or approval of the earlier result.
4. Propagate the exact changed contract, revision, proof seam, and required next action to every affected child owner. Preserve unaffected child scope, ownership, claim, evidence, and progress.
5. Re-evaluate parent coverage and integration. Parent ownership never moves to a child, and child completion never proves the parent Outcome.
6. For a Durable system change, link the material transition to its Change and Legacy Record. Inline work keeps the same meanings in the conversation and crosses to Durable coordination before state must survive the interaction.

A bounded authorized exception that changes no accepted contract, result, or proof obligation needs only its scope, duration, consequence, and recovery trigger recorded; it does not manufacture a new revision or invalidate unrelated evidence. An unauthorized override remains a blocked proposal under the same classifier. Preserve the exact result and request the authorized decision; perform no guarded effect.

## Interrupt and resume safely

Treat the work unit before switching attention:

- **Continuous work:** stop at a coherent checkpoint and preserve source and result revisions, owner, completed range, pickup point, next action, and proof still required.
- **Atomic work before its effect boundary:** stop before the boundary and preserve the exact proposal, Review, approval, preconditions, and recheck action.
- **Atomic work crossing its effect boundary:** do not silently interrupt it. Observe the accepted effect or failure, record the exact result, and enter verification or recovery before applying the new message.
- **Changed approved work:** invalidate affected Review and approval before any effect. Recheck approval validity, expiry, revocation, conditions, and exact revision immediately before resuming.

If the switch crosses a Persistence boundary, materialize continuation through the selected Adapter. When that capability is unavailable, remain visibly Degraded and require an accepted handoff or restored durable capability before leaving state behind.

## Complete the Inline lifecycle

Immediate Review evaluates the exact result against the semantic Specification and governing standards. Then verify any real effect and the parent Outcome itself. Keep required propagation, recovery, or follow-up inside the Outcome; optional improvements may become related Outcomes.

Return the smallest user-visible result that proves the accepted Outcome. The conversation is sufficient evidence only while its complete contract and proof remain available in the current interaction.

An authorized request to terminate the active Outcome without a successor uses the explicit replacement or supersession classification to enter cancellation. Record the authorizer, rationale, partial effects, retained evidence, every child and dependency disposition, and recovery or reversal needs. Cancellation satisfies no dependent requirement unless that dependent owner explicitly replaces, waives, or cancels it.
