# Sources and further reading

Use this repository index when a named concept needs its original discipline, evidence limits, or deeper explanation. These sources inform the suite. They do not replace direct evidence about the system under examination.

## Systems, boundaries, and interfaces

- [SEBoK: Engineered System Context](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Engineered_System_Context): system context and boundaries
- [SEBoK: Introduction to System Fundamentals](https://sebokwiki.org/wiki/Introduction_to_System_Fundamentals): recursive systems and containing wholes
- [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf): system lifecycle and interfaces

## Causal inquiry and intervention

- [Lean Enterprise Institute: Five Whys](https://www.lean.org/lexicon-terms/5-whys/) and [Card: limitations of Five Whys](https://qualitysafety.bmj.com/content/26/8/671): causal inquiry and the limits of single-root-cause stories
- [Donella Meadows: Leverage Points](https://donellameadows.org/archives/leverage-points-places-to-intervene-in-a-system/): intervention points with disproportionate effect
- [Herbert Simon: bounded rationality and satisficing](https://doi.org/10.2307/1884852): adequate choices under bounded search
- [NIST: Value of Information and Decision Pathways](https://www.nist.gov/publications/value-information-and-decision-pathways-concepts-and-case-studies): whether more information is worth its cost
- [Institute of Culinary Education: mise en place](https://www.ice.edu/blog/lesson-1-mise-en-place): readiness for the next operation; this suite adds a stopping rule

## Economics and affected systems

- [OpenStax: choices under a budget constraint](https://openstax.org/books/principles-microeconomics-2e/pages/2-1-how-individuals-make-choices-based-on-their-budget-constraint): feasible sets and opportunity cost
- [OpenStax: costs in the short run](https://openstax.org/books/principles-microeconomics-3e/pages/7-3-costs-in-the-short-run) and [costs in the long run](https://openstax.org/books/principles-microeconomics-3e/pages/7-5-costs-in-the-long-run): cost structure across scale and time
- [IMF: Externalities](https://www.imf.org/external/pubs/ft/fandd/basics/38-externalities.htm): effects omitted from the deciding party's trade-off
- [Ronald Coase: The Problem of Social Cost](https://www.law.uchicago.edu/lawecon/coaseinmemoriam/problemofsocialcost): reciprocal effects and transaction costs

## Friction and judgment

- [Emil Kowalski: Friction as a Feature](https://emilkowal.ski/ui/friction-as-a-feature): friction can preserve a decision function when production becomes cheap

## Representation formats

- [HTML Standard: custom data attributes](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/dom.html#embedding-custom-non-visible-data-with-the-data-*-attributes): extensible machine-readable semantics in HTML
- [Mermaid flowcharts](https://mermaid.js.org/syntax/flowchart.html): nodes, decision shapes, and labeled edges
- [n8n flow logic](https://docs.n8n.io/flow-logic/): conditionals, loops, waits, merges, and sub-workflows in a node-and-connection interface

## Evidence, operation, and change

- [NIST SP 800-160 Vol. 2 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/160/v2/r1/final): resilience and recovery
- [Google SRE Workbook: Monitoring](https://sre.google/workbook/monitoring/): monitoring tied to a decision
- [Deming Institute: PDSA](https://deming.org/explore/pdsa/): learning from a stated theory and observed result
- [Campbell: measurement corruption under target pressure](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED178584.pdf): proxy corruption under target pressure
