# Agent Skills

Shared language for authoring and publishing the active skill suite.

## Language

**Active skill**:
A skill under `skills/` that belongs to the current suite and may be published or invoked.

**Archived skill**:
A skill under `old skills/` retained only as historical reference.

**Systems skills suite**:
The focused active skills that share the language and principles established by Thinking in Systems.

**Work tracker**:
The system that stores and relates records of planned work. It may be a hosted service or local Markdown.

**Language record**:
A `CONTEXT.md` file containing the accepted language for one context.

**Prototype**:
A reversible experiment that produces evidence to answer one question by testing one hypothesis.

**ADR**:
A durable record of an accepted decision and why it was made. An ADR records a decision; it does not make one.
