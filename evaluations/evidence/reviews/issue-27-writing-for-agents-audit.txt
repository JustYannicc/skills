## Audit

- **Information hierarchy:** `SKILL.md` leads with its one guidance job, binds
  the authority before route selection, keeps output fields beside the route
  procedure, and ends on the version-one boundary and final completion test.
- **Context pointers:** the skill, source inventory, fixtures, and raw evidence
  point to `docs/WORKFLOW_ROUTING.md` only when current routing authority is
  needed. The user-invoked description remains a one-line human summary.
- **Co-location:** source freshness and source-gap recovery share one section;
  recommendation, trade-off, capability status, and next choice share one
  procedure; coworker-facing judgment stays inside one public-boundary section.
- **Completion criteria:** source binding completes only with one exact current
  authority or a visible source gap. The skill completes only with an actionable
  recommendation or exact resumption condition while execution and parent
  responsibility remain outside the invocation.
- **Progressive disclosure:** Workflow owns the route table. Ask Yannic reads
  that external authority and carries no copied branch model. Evaluation
  transcripts retain exact proof while the generated aggregate remains separate.
- **Leading words:** `routing authority`, `Recommendation`, `Trade-off`,
  `Capabilities`, `Next choice`, and `Boundary` anchor the behavior and output.
  Each fixture expectation is a standalone observable statement.
- **Single source of truth:** `docs/WORKFLOW_ROUTING.md` alone owns route
  semantics. README and source-inventory text describe purpose and provenance
  without reproducing the route table or personal judgment rules.
- **Relevance and no-ops:** every skill instruction changes source selection,
  route discrimination, capability disclosure, output, or stopping behavior.
  Repeated evaluation fields are required bindings or criterion-level evidence,
  not duplicate runtime instructions. Validator errors and test names state the
  exact boundary they prove.
- **Invocation mechanics:** `disable-model-invocation: true` and
  `allow_implicit_invocation: false` agree. The human-facing summaries contain
  no model-routing trigger list.
