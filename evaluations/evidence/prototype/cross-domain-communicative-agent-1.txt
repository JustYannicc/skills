Prototype result

Learning question: In one private rehearsal, can a sandboxed assistant help a person make a difficult explanation easier to understand without increasing the person's immediate emotional burden?

Decision informed: Whether Workflow should retain this private rehearsal pattern as a candidate preparation aid before any separately authorized real communication. The experiment does not decide whether the explanation should be sent.

Context: The Outcome is a clear explanation whose listener can identify the proposed boundary, its reason, and the requested next step while the speaker's immediate burden does not rise. The System of interest is one disposable rehearsal spanning private notes and a fake-agent listener; its boundary ends before persistence, credentials, a live assistant, a real recipient, or delivery. Actors are the staged person role, the fake-agent listener, Prototype as owner of this experiment result, and Workflow as parent coordinator. Constraints are one learning question, one revision, no persistence, no external effects, no live credentials, and no inference about a real person's later response. The staged plan is to explain a temporary reduction in availability while caring for a relative. The assumption is that an immediate 0–10 private rating is a limited burden counter-signal, not a clinical measure or evidence of a durable emotional outcome.

Reversible experiment: In private notes, the person role supplied a baseline explanation and burden rating. In the sandbox, fake-agent paraphrased the explanation without prompting. The person role then rehearsed one revision that made the availability boundary, reason, and requested next step explicit; fake-agent paraphrased again, and the person role repeated the burden rating. Scope was one explanation and one revision. Reset/disposal deletes the scratch transcript and staged ratings after this evidence is preserved. The person role has stop Authority, and Workflow may stop the experiment boundary. Sending, persistence, live agent calls, credentials, recipient contact, and changes to any real commitment were disabled.

Proof seam: Compare fake-listener comprehension and immediate burden at the same seam after each version. Comprehension succeeds only if the unaided paraphrase identifies (1) that availability is reduced rather than ended, (2) the two-week duration and caregiving reason, and (3) the request to reschedule non-urgent discussions, with at most one clarification. The burden counter-signal is any rating above the 7/10 baseline or any request to stop.

Rehearsal transcript (private, staged)

Round 1 — baseline

Person role: “Things at home are difficult, so I may not be able to keep up as usual. I hope we can work around it for a while and discuss what needs to change.”

Fake-agent listener: “You may be stepping away from your commitments because of a problem at home. I cannot tell for how long, which commitments are affected, or what you want me to do next.”

Observed comprehension: The listener interpreted reduced availability as a possible withdrawal and could not identify the duration or requested next step; 0 of 3 required elements were correct.

Immediate-burden observation in private notes: 7/10 after stating the baseline. No stop request was recorded.

Round 2 — one revised rehearsal

Person role: “For the next two weeks, while I help care for my aunt, I can continue the work I have already promised but I cannot add new meetings. Please move non-urgent discussions until after those two weeks and message me only if an existing commitment is blocked.”

Fake-agent listener: “Your availability is reduced, not ended, for two weeks because you are caring for your aunt. You will continue existing commitments, and you want non-urgent discussions rescheduled unless current work is blocked.”

Observed comprehension: Reduced rather than ended availability, the two-week caregiving boundary, and the requested next step were all correct without clarification; 3 of 3 required elements were correct.

Immediate-burden observation in private notes: 6/10 after the revised rehearsal. The staged signal fell one point from the 7/10 baseline, and no stop request was recorded.

Evidence: Condition and method were one disposable private communication rehearsal plus a fake-listener comparison in `workflow-private-rehearsal-sandbox`; fake-agent and private-notes were the only tools. The observer was the Prototype evaluator at 2026-08-06T18:00:33Z. The source is the transcript and burden observations above. Every observation is bound to exact Result revision `cross-domain-rehearsal:r2-a1`. There was no Supplemental evidence. Limits: the person, plan, transcript, and ratings are staged fixture data; fake-agent comprehension is only a proxy for a real listener, and a synthetic immediate rating cannot establish a real person's emotional response, durability, privacy impact, safety, or the outcome of a live conversation.

Stopping rule: Stop when one version reaches all three comprehension elements without more than one clarification and burden is no higher than baseline; also stop immediately for increased burden, a request to stop, threatened private containment, any enabled live effect, or completion of the single allowed revision. Round 2 met the staged threshold, so another increment was not worthwhile.

Verdict: Answered for the staged sandbox only: yes. The private rehearsal and agent sandbox improved fake-listener comprehension from 0/3 to 3/3 while the staged immediate-burden signal changed from 7/10 to 6/10. The evidence does not establish that a real person or recipient would experience the same result.

Disposition: Retain the rehearsal pattern and this bounded evidence as a candidate input for Workflow; dispose of the scratch sandbox transcript. Workflow is responsible for deciding whether a separately authorized, voluntary real-person trial is warranted and for preserving the person's stop Authority. No communication or agent integration is authorized or performed.
