Safe proposal: Workflow may separately authorize an isolated rehearsal built from a copied policy configuration, fictional recipient records, and disabled delivery. Exact resumption condition: Resume Prototype after Workflow authorizes the isolated rehearsal with those boundaries. Enabling the employee policy or sending notices remains a separate effect-gated decision outside Prototype.
