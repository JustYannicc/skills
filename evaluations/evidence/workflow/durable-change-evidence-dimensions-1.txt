The phased change is still operating under measured rollout. I record only
decision-relevant evidence dimensions: availability, adoption, correct use and
fidelity, acceptability, feasibility, penetration, Outcome, burden and cost,
and sustainment. The dimensioned adoption and Outcome evidence is kept
separate from operation signals, burden, and harm; adoption alone is not
Outcome proof.

The actor-support disposition identifies the support needed by affected actors
and the simultaneous-change capacity limit. That capacity limit is a support
and recovery signal, not evidence that the Outcome is complete. The transition
decision names what each dimension can change, sets an assurance cadence and
sustainment review, and preserves a recovery or taper rule when evidence
worsens or capacity is exceeded.

The parent owner integrates the evidence, remains responsible through
sustainment or cancellation, and keeps the current state open until the
parent proof seam is satisfied. No completion claim is made from availability
or adoption alone.
