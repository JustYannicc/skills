## Inline handoff contract

This is a bounded, one-time System interaction. Its System of interest is the source-comparison handoff; its containing System is the active Workflow. Nonrecurrence removes any need for a durable System Record, but not for a governed handoff.

- **Intent and good-enough Outcome:** obtain a comparison that gives Workflow sufficient, source-grounded evidence to make the next decision. Good enough means the assigned sources are compared against the stated criteria, material uncertainty and disagreement are explicit, and the result can be checked against the supplied sources.
- **Boundary and Authority:** Workflow may delegate only the defined comparison. The subagent may read the assigned sources, analyze them, and return a bounded written result; it may not choose the parent direction, alter scope, make external effects, or treat its conclusion as an approval. Workflow retains phase selection, coordination, integration, and terminal proof. The same agent follows both Thinking in Systems and Workflow; they are capabilities, not separate actors.
- **Actors and responsibility:** Workflow is the parent owner and remains responsible for the integrated Outcome. The subagent accepts responsibility only for the assigned comparison and its evidence. The subagent's completion, delay, or failure does not transfer or end parent Ownership.
- **Input and preconditions:** send the exact source set or locations, comparison question and criteria, required depth, relevant context, output format, deadline or return trigger, and authority limits. If any of these are materially ambiguous, the subagent returns the smallest discriminating question before proceeding.
- **Output and proof:** return source identifiers or quotations sufficient to locate each claim, the comparison by criterion, material assumptions or gaps, and a clear status: complete, partial, blocked, or conflicting. The parent verifies that the result covers the assigned scope and that the cited evidence supports its claims, then integrates it with the other Workflow evidence. This parent verification is the completion proof.
- **Failure and recovery:** on missing access, unclear criteria, unavailable sources, interruption, or conflicting evidence, preserve the received inputs and partial findings; make no broader inference or effect; return the blocker, affected scope, and smallest safe next action. Workflow may clarify, narrow, replace the source, or reassign the bounded task; it rechecks the returned result against the original assignment before integration.

The handoff is ready for Workflow delegation. No durable state, change/legacy treatment, ambient operation, metric, or informed exception is required for this reversible one-time comparison; Workflow should retain the inline contract and the exact returned result for this interaction's proof.
