Treat this as a bounded System interaction, even though it will occur only once. It needs an inline handoff contract, not a Durable System Record.

**Handoff contract**

- **System boundary:** one bounded source comparison from dispatch to the parent’s integration of the returned result. The active `Workflow` skill remains the same agent’s coordinator; it is not a separate agent.
- **Outcome and proof:** a comparison that answers the stated question with source-linked findings, material disagreements or gaps, and a clear recommendation or bounded uncertainty. The parent owner verifies that the exact result covers the assigned sources and criteria, checks the citations and reasoning, and integrates it into the parent work; the child’s submission alone is not completion.
- **Input and preconditions:** the exact sources or source locations, comparison question and criteria, required scope, format, deadline or response expectation if relevant, and any constraints on access, privacy, or citations. Preserve the supplied source revisions.
- **Child responsibility and Authority:** the subagent accepts responsibility only for reading the assigned sources, comparing them against the stated criteria, and returning the bounded analysis with evidence and uncertainty. It may not change scope, approve decisions, make external effects, or present a conclusion as an approved parent decision.
- **Parent Ownership and Authority:** the Workflow-coordinating agent retains ownership of the integrated Outcome, scope decisions, integration, recovery, and terminal proof. Delegating this bounded duty does not transfer parent Ownership or coordination.
- **Output:** source-by-source comparison, exact supporting references, material conflicts or missing evidence, assumptions, and the child’s proposed conclusion; no unapproved effects.
- **Failure and return:** if a source is unavailable, the scope is ambiguous, evidence conflicts materially, or the child cannot complete the comparison, preserve the work completed and exact gap, return it to the parent, and pause that bounded duty. The parent decides whether to clarify, narrow scope, replace the source, or stop; it must recheck the returned result and assumptions before resuming.

This clears the ready-for-delegation threshold: the one-time handoff has a bounded input, output, Authority, proof seam, and recovery path. Return this contract to `Workflow` for dispatch and coordination.
