## Governing constraints for Workflow

- Preserve each case’s original intake data, prior classification, rule/version, rationale, and timestamps. A migration may add a new classification and disposition, but must not rewrite history as though the new rule always applied.
- Open a proposed successor System Record and a linked, versioned Change and Legacy Record before adoption. The latter must name the current and proposed rule versions, decision owner, approval, affected sources/interfaces, recovery and rollback contract, and predecessor-retirement conditions.
- The exact replacement rule, its intended outcome and good-enough threshold, authoritative owner, approval boundary, and cutoff interpretation remain unresolved. Workflow must surface these as decision items; no model confidence or calendar deadline supplies authority.

Classify every discoverable case as `unaffected`, `compatible`, `auto-adapt`, `review-required`, `conflict`, `exempt`, or `unreachable/unknown`. Unknown coverage must remain visible rather than being assumed migrated.
- Specify the proposed transformation or re-evaluation path per cohort, including how conflicts and exceptions are decided, where unresolved cases remain visible, and which state is authoritative during transition. Do not silently strand cases under the former contract.
- Define operational modes before rollout:
  - **Normal:** apply the approved new rule to eligible new cases and perform the approved, bounded legacy disposition.
  - **Degraded:** preserve incoming cases and original data; use only a pre-authorized minimum path (for example, queue clearly marked cases for review) when required sources, capacity, or dependencies are unavailable.
  - **Paused:** stop rule application and migration effects when the rule, authority, data integrity, or effect gate is unresolved; preserve state and expose the blocking gap.
  - **Recovery:** triage failed or stale cohorts, reconcile authoritative state, resolve stale assumptions and conflicts, repair or roll back bounded changes, verify the healthy baseline, then resume in bounded steps without creating an unbounded backlog.

Test representative new, legacy, ambiguous, exception, missing-data, interrupted, and rollback cases. Record outcomes, mismatch rates, burden, and evidence gaps; simulation success alone does not authorize rollout.
- Establish rollout, monitoring, reconciliation, rollback/repair, and resumption criteria. The live application must be resumable and bounded, with receipts and post-change verification. Define what evidence would pause or reverse the change, and retain an inspectable, human-readable projection of status, assumptions, cohort results, failures, and decisions.
- Obtain the required operator approval binding the exact successor rule, legacy plan, authority envelope, and rollout/rollback revision. The agent may draft and simulate these artifacts but may not promote the rule, expand authority, or change cases.
