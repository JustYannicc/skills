## Governance assessment

This is a material change to an existing durable System, not a rule edit. It changes the governing classification contract for future intake and affects legacy cases created under the previous contract.

The requested Outcome is: every new and existing case is handled under a clearly defined successor classification policy, with no silent loss of history, unsupported reclassification, or unowned transition burden.

Before any effect, preserve:

- the exact old rule, proposed replacement rule, their versions, and each case’s original classification;
- the affected population, including truthful unknown coverage;
- the owner and explicit Authority for approval, activation, external effects, and legacy disposition;
- the conditions under which an old classification is historical evidence versus an active operational state.

A material ambiguity remains: “including cases created under the old rule” could mean either apply the new rule to future handling of those cases, or overwrite their historical classifications. Do not choose between those meanings without Authority. The default governing constraint is: preserve historical classifications and record a successor classification or disposition; never rewrite history to imply the new rule always applied.

The successor contract needs versioned rule identity, deterministic eligibility and transition checks, an LLM only where interpretation of unstructured facts is genuinely required, and a visible exception route for ambiguous cases. It must define normal operation, unavailable inputs or approvals, pause conditions, recovery, rollback or repair, and ownership of stale or failed reclassification.

Proposed legacy disposition:

| Cohort | Proposed disposition |
| --- | --- |
| New cases after cutover | Apply the successor rule from the effective version onward. |
| Existing cases whose classification is still operational and safely recomputable | Simulate the successor classification; auto-adapt only if approved equivalence and evidence rules pass. |
| Existing cases with material ambiguity, changed rights/obligations, external effects, or incomplete inputs | `review-required`; preserve the old result and surface the proposed successor result with rationale. |
| Finalized, audited, or historically meaningful old classifications | Preserve as historical facts; create a linked current disposition rather than overwrite. |
| Incompatible cases | `conflict`; block automatic transition and route to the named decision Authority. |
| Exempt cases | `exempt`, with the reason and review condition recorded. |
| Cases that cannot be located or evaluated | `unreachable/unknown`; report coverage truthfully and define a recheck or repair path. |

The change needs a successor System Record and linked Change and Legacy Record before activation. They must state the transition boundary, cohort rules, rollout and cutover conditions, rollback or repair path, retained history, support and recovery ownership, and retirement state of the old rule.

Proof obligations before activation:

- simulate the production-intended classification and render the exact proposed changes without durable writes or external effects;
- cover normal, ambiguous, missing-input, old-rule legacy, conflict, degraded, recovery, and rollback scenarios;
- verify correct rule selection and preserved history separately from validating that the new policy improves its intended Outcome;
- measure burden and harm, including review volume, incorrect transitions, unresolved cases, support cost, and any affected-party impact;
- bind approval to the exact successor rule, case-selection logic, assumptions, and simulation revision.

`'Workflow'` should coordinate the change only after these governing constraints, Authority boundaries, legacy dispositions, and evidence requirements are accepted.
