## Governance assessment for Workflow

**Classification:** This is a recurring customer-update process with external-message effects, approval state, and recovery obligations; govern it as a Durable system. The Workflow skill remains owner of coordination, integration, and terminal proof. This assessment supplies constraints only; it neither sends a message nor completes the parent Outcome.

**Outcome and boundary:** Produce a customer update that is accurate, approved by the authorized approver, and sent only as the exact approved draft. Good enough is one identifiable draft revision with a recorded approval that matches it exactly and visible evidence of the send result. The process covers drafting, revision identity, approval, effect gating, and recovery; customer communication strategy, approval policy, and recipient authorization remain with their authorized owners. Assumptions needing confirmation before live use include the approving Actor, recipient source, retention/privacy rules, and the governing approval policy.

**Authority and handoffs:**

- The LLM may compose or revise a proposed draft and surface uncertainty; it has no authority to approve or send.
- A designated human approver may approve or reject one exact draft revision under the applicable policy.
- A deterministic approval-and-send guard must verify the approved draft ID or content hash, approver identity, approval status, recipient scope, and current policy before enabling the send effector. Any material edit, recipient change, or stale approval invalidates approval and returns the draft to review.
- The send mechanism may perform only the validated effect and must record the exact revision, approval reference, timestamp, recipient result, and failure state. Workflow consumes that evidence and remains responsible for follow-up.

**State and operating rules:** Preserve the source facts, each draft revision, approval/rejection decision, approver, effect request, delivery result, and error history under one writable authority per type. Treat the customer-facing draft as a derived representation, never as approval state. In normal operation, draft → exact approval → validated send is the only effect path. If approval, policy, dependency, or proof is missing, enter Paused: preserve the draft and block sending. On delivery uncertainty or failure, enter Degraded/Recovery: expose the status, prevent an unverified retry or duplicate send, reconcile against the delivery provider, then resume only with evidence of the outcome or a newly approved revision.

**Proof and learning:** Before enabling effects, simulate the production path with effectors disabled for an approved draft, a changed-after-approval draft, missing approval, stale approval, unavailable sender, ambiguous recipient scope, and recovery after an uncertain delivery. The public proof seam is the immutable link from sent message to the exact approved draft revision. Measure correct approval-to-send binding, delivery/reconciliation outcomes, and review burden; do not treat send volume as the Outcome. Preserve existing drafts and approvals during any future process change, re-evaluate their compatibility, and require a successor contract and transition record for material changes.

**Workflow decision:** Select or confirm the approver, approval policy, recipient authority, state owner, retention rules, and send-provider reconciliation behavior. Once those are recorded, Workflow can proceed with a bounded design or simulation; until then, composing drafts without external effects is safe, while sending is blocked.
