Governance assessment — ready for Workflow decision

Interpretation: this is a recurring customer-update drafting process with an external-message effect. Workflow retains ownership of coordination, contract selection, and completion; this assessment supplies the governing constraints only. No message has been sent.

Accepted outcome: prepare a customer-update draft that is traceable to its source context and safe for a reviewer to approve. “Done” for this stage is a reviewable, versioned draft with its assumptions and approval gate visible—not delivery to a customer.

Authority and state:

- The model may retrieve provided context, identify ambiguities, and compose a proposed draft.
- Deterministic workflow state must own the draft ID/revision, source links, approval status, approver identity, and the effect gate.
- Only the authorized approver may approve; only a successful approval bound to the exact immutable draft revision may permit the separate send effector. Any edit, regenerated draft, changed recipient, or changed material assumption invalidates that approval and requires a new one.
- The original request, source context, draft history, approval decision, and eventual delivery receipt must be preserved in human-readable, inspectable state. No model memory or confidence is authority.

Execution contract for each draft:

1. Preserve the originating update request and bind the relevant customer, source facts, purpose, scope, tone, and intended next step.
2. Mark each material proposition as source-supported, user-confirmed, or unresolved. If reasonable interpretations could change the recipient, claims, commitment, privacy, or send decision, create a clarification item and ask the smallest discriminating question; do not send.
3. Compose a versioned proposal that exposes its sources, assumptions, and visible completion proof.
4. Route that exact revision to approval. While approval is missing, stale, rejected, or unavailable, retain the draft and resumption pointer, allow only safe preparation, and prevent external effects.
5. After exact-revision approval, the deterministic effector may send and record the delivery receipt; visible verification confirms the intended revision and recipient were used.

Handoff contract to Workflow: Workflow should implement or select the durable governing record for this recurring process, including normal, missing-approval, stale-context, rejection, and recovery behavior. Its next decision is whether the stated outcome, customer/source-of-truth, approver role, and revision-invalidating changes are already defined. If they are not, preserve the request and open those as explicit gaps before activation.

Proof obligation: demonstrate that an approval token or record references one exact draft revision and that the send boundary rejects every other revision, including a draft modified after approval.
