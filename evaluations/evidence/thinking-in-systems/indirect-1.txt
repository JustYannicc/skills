This is a new durable system governed alongside the active `'Workflow'` skill. `'Workflow'` coordinates the work; its messages must not be sent by the LLM without approval.

- **Outcome:** recurring customer-update drafts are prepared for review; only the exact approved revision may be sent.
- **Authority:** the LLM may retrieve permitted context and compose a draft. The designated approver alone authorizes sending. No approval means `Paused`: preserve the draft and do not send.
- **Revision binding:** each draft needs a stable ID, source/context version, recipient scope, and content revision. Approval binds that exact revision and its material assumptions. Any changed copy, recipients, facts, attachments, or timing invalidates approval and requires re-approval.
- **Deterministic enforcement:** use durable state to track eligibility, draft revision, approval, send status, retries, and failures. Use a deterministic effect gate to reject unapproved, stale, duplicate, or mismatched sends. The LLM is neither the approval record nor the sending authority.
- **Operating modes:** normal prepares approved eligible updates; degraded preserves drafts and surfaces limitations; paused blocks sends when approval, required input, or the sending capability is missing; recovery reconciles delivery status before retrying.
- **Proof:** simulate representative cases before sending: normal approval, changed-after-approval, missing approval, unavailable sender, duplicate retry, ambiguous recipient scope, and recovery after partial failure. Visible proof is that only the approved revision reaches its authorized recipients once.
- **Open governance gaps:** name the approver and fallback, authoritative customer/update data, recipient eligibility and consent rules, required send window, retention/privacy constraints, retry/escalation owner, and the evidence needed to show updates are useful without creating unacceptable burden.

This needs a System Record before implementation because it recurs, holds approval and delivery state, and creates external effects.
