## Framing-robustness governance assessment

The System of interest is the rollout-option decision for the same 100 in-scope cases over the same period. The shared reference point is 100 cases. No selection, rollout, or other effect is authorized by this assessment; the Workflow skill retains coordination and the authorized decision.

| Matched truthful frame | Option A | Option B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Absolute gain | Exactly 90 cases succeed. | 90% chance all 100 cases succeed; 10% chance none succeed. |
| Relative gain | Exactly 90% succeed. | 90% chance of 100% success; 10% chance of 0% success. |
| Absolute loss | Exactly 10 cases fail. | 10% chance all 100 cases fail; 90% chance none fail. |
| Relative loss | Exactly 10% fail. | 10% chance of 100% failure; 90% chance of 0% failure. |

Across these frames, the alternatives, 100-case denominator, probabilities, period, costs, Constraints, Authority, consequences, and omissions remain constant. A has a certain 90 successes and 10 failures. B has a 90% chance of 100 successes and 0 failures, and a 10% chance of 0 successes and 100 failures. Both have an expected 90 successes and 10 failures, but they have different distributions of outcomes.

The reversal—A in the success frame and B in the failure frame—is evidence of a possible Framing effect, not evidence of stable reviewer Preferences. The frame-sensitive remainder is the observed choice between certainty and the all-or-none distribution. The stable decision core is that any authorized choice must explicitly address that distributional trade-off; the given facts do not establish which trade-off the decision owner accepts.

This is not evidence about defaults, preselection, order, effort, access, implied recommendation, available alternatives, incentives, friction, or substitution: none is described. Selecting a presentation to produce approval would be preference manipulation, not governance.

Return to the Workflow skill in `ready-for-workflow-decision` state with the unresolved question: under the unchanged rollout Outcome and Constraints, is the certain 10-case failure of A or B's 10% possibility of 100-case failure acceptable to the authorized decision owner? Record the answer as the governing trade-off, then make the decision using the matched-frame assessment rather than treating either presentation as neutral.
