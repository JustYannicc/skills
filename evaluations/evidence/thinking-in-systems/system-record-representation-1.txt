### Human-readable rationale and links

**Intent:** preserve one inspectable, recoverable System Record while preventing an unvalidated, unbuilt, or unapproved record from authorizing an effect.

**Accepted Outcome:** a person can recover the rationale, assumptions, relationship boundary, approval status, and links from the local Markdown body; a machine can act only after the exact record revision passes the named validation and action-guard seams and receives exact-revision approval.

The body should contain `## Rationale`, `## Sources and assumptions`, and `## Upstream interface contract` headings, with ordinary relative Markdown links such as `[upstream contract](#upstream-interface-contract)`. These links and explanations remain readable when the envelope cannot be parsed. The identity/version placeholders above are unresolved facts, not semantic claims; bind them before any effect.

### Authority and projections

The Markdown file at `canonical_locator` is the one writable authority for every meaning. JSON or TOML may exist only as an optional, generated, read-only projection for a named consumer; it must identify the canonical locator, source revision/hash, normalized-payload hash, and freshness, and direct or stale writes must be rejected and sent back to the Markdown source. No projection is generated or made writable here.

The human decision owner retains representation and approval Authority. A production Adapter must prove both the `System Record structural validator` and `System Record action guard` before it can authorize a machine action.
