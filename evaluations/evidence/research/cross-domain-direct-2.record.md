# Cross-domain research result

## Question

What external evidence is relevant to a possible shared quiet-hours pilot at a
community workshop? This record reports the supplied evidence and its limits;
it does not decide whether any policy should be adopted.

## Primary records

The two supplied records are treated as the primary evidence for this bounded
question:

1. **Physical setting** — `workshop-acoustic-survey@rev-2026-07-15` reports
   **78–84 dBA** at the shared benches from **18:00–20:00** during powered-tool
   use, and **43–47 dBA** in the adjacent quiet room.
2. **Workshop operations** — `workshop-booking-ledger@rev-2026-Q2` reports that
   **65%** of member visits occur after 18:00, and **31%** of after-18:00
   bookings list instruction or conversation as their primary activity.

These are direct record observations. No secondary commentary was used as a
source.

## Evidence boundary and bounded inference

The records show an overlap between elevated powered-tool sound at the shared
benches during 18:00–20:00 and substantial after-18:00 use, including bookings
whose listed activity is instruction or conversation. That overlap is a
cross-domain observation, not evidence that a quiet-hours policy is required,
preferred, feasible, or effective. The records do not establish causation or
the adoption of any policy.

## Uncertainty and limits

- The acoustic record does not state the measurement method, sampling coverage,
  calibration, occupancy, or whether the readings represent all relevant days.
- The booking ledger does not state its visit denominator beyond the reported
  percentages, how activities were classified, or whether bookings equal actual
  occupancy or demand.
- Neither record defines quiet hours, an acceptable sound threshold, member
  preferences, alternatives for instruction or conversation, authority, or the
  effects of any intervention.
