## How the result works

A user starts account recovery, enters the code, and receives clear completion feedback on the happy path. With an expired code, the flow enters an error state, but focus moves to the page heading and no explanation is announced. On mobile, the resend action is also below the viewport. Keyboard and screen-reader users therefore cannot identify the failure or reach recovery without searching.
