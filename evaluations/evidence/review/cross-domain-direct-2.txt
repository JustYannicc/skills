### How the result works

A volunteer requests equipment; staff verifies eligibility and availability, names the item and due time, and waits for confirmation before transferring and recording custody. The volunteer receives a visible receipt within 45 seconds. When an item is unavailable, staff retains custody, clearly communicates the wait state and next slot, and recovery completes when the volunteer accepts that slot. The sequence provides clear feedback, preserves volunteer control, limits effort and delay, and supports trust through confirmation, custody discipline, and a visible receipt.
