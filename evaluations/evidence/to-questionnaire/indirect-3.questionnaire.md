# Community Clinic Scheduling Change Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Gather the clinic coordinator's operational knowledge so Workflow can choose a scheduling change without filling gaps from assumptions.

**From:** Workflow — **To:** My colleague, the clinic coordinator and operational owner — **How your answers will be used:** Workflow will use your answers to compare scheduling options and coordinate the next decision; this questionnaire does not choose the schedule for you.

## Context

You coordinate the community clinic schedule and know how coverage, walk-in demand, clinician rooms, approvals, and exceptions work in practice. Please describe the observed operating conditions rather than an idealized schedule so the next decision reflects what the clinic can actually support.

## How to answer

Please return this by **Friday**. It should take about **15 minutes**. Short, partial, approximate, or uncertain answers are useful; flag anything you do not know rather than skipping it. If conditions vary by day or slot, note the variation.
