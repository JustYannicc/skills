# Approval Handoff Discovery Questionnaire

**Purpose:** Understand where and why the approval handoff stalls so the requesting team member can choose an appropriate fix.

**From:** Requesting team member — **To:** Operations lead (colleague; owner of the approval handoff) — **How your answers will be used:** Your observations will be returned to the requesting workflow to diagnose the handoff and choose a fix; this questionnaire does not choose the fix.

## Context

You own the approval handoff and have direct operational knowledge of where it slows or stops. I am mapping the observed process, its exceptions, and its effects so the next decision is based on what actually happens rather than assumptions.

## How to answer

Please reply by **Friday**. The questionnaire should take about **15 minutes**. Short, partial, approximate, or uncertain answers are useful; flag anything you do not know rather than skipping it.
