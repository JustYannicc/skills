## Cultural influences and maintainer principles

- Nemik's manifesto in *Andor*: the line-forward metaphor. Any quotation must remain short and attributed.
- “Everyone will not just”: a system depending on universal repeated compliance is not a complete solution.
- “How you do anything is how you do everything”: used as an informed-exception prompt, never as coercion or shame.
- The axe-sharpening stories: preparation has high leverage but needs a stopping rule.
