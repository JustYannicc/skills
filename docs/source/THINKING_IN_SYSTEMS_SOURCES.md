# Thinking in Systems source record

This repository-facing record preserves provenance, evidence classification, and source links used to maintain the public Thinking in Systems skill. It is intentionally outside the installed skill's agent-facing references because none of this material is required to apply the method.

## Governing history

The installed standard is adapted from the public-safe Thinking in Systems v1.0 method at [`d078ee7`](https://github.com/JustYannicc/skills/blob/d078ee7a22f92600eb275f27f5a700e5bf37d778/docs/source/THINKING_IN_SYSTEMS_STANDARD.md). The current skill preserves its transferable governing behavior while removing maintainer history, publication state, fixed biographical examples, and source debate from runtime instructions.

The private originals remain outside this public repository. Their fingerprints are recorded in the [source archive](README.md). They do not override the current public skill.

## Evidence classes

- **Normative design choices:** definitions, Authority boundaries, proportional persistence, informed exceptions, and the one-job relationship with the `'Workflow'` skill.
- **Empirical influences:** evidence about systems engineering, resilience, incentives, behavior, decision-making, evaluation, and learning. Sources support hypotheses and guardrails; they do not make every System causal claim universal.
- **Historical influences:** practitioner concepts and maintainer principles that helped expose useful questions. Runtime guidance keeps the transferable rule rather than the biography, slogan, or fixed example.
- **Behavioral evidence:** public fixtures and observations under `evaluations/` prove invocation, application, and the no-takeover boundary at the installed skill's public seams.
